## Mx. Voice 4.4.0 beta 2

This is a **beta** of Mx. Voice 4.4. It introduces **Shows**, tracks what you've played during the night, and gives the app a fresher look. Please try it before a real show, and tell us what breaks or confuses you.

> **Before you upgrade:** 4.4 changes how your layout is kept. Changes made during a show are now kept **only if you save them** (Shows → Save). Quitting no longer saves your hotkeys and holding tank automatically. Read "Your layout is now a show" below.

To get beta updates in the app, turn on **Preferences → Include prerelease updates (beta/alpha versions)**. Beta installs are offered newer betas and the final 4.4.0 release.

---

### Your layout is now a show

- The hotkeys and holding tank you had become a show named **Default Show**. You can rename it.
- A **show** is a saved hotkey and holding-tank layout. Each profile can have as many as you like: one per venue, team or format.
- **Changes are kept only when you save them.** Use **Shows → Save** to update the show on screen, or **Save As** to make a new one.
- **Quitting or switching profiles doesn't save.** The show opens next time as you last saved it.
- **The header shows your profile and the loaded show.** A **dot** after the show name means the layout on screen has unsaved changes.
- **Revert to Saved** puts the show back as you last saved it. It's available only while there are unsaved changes.
- **Switching to another show, or importing one, asks** whether to save your changes first.
- **Rename and Delete act on the show on screen.** To tidy another show, switch to it first.
- **Shows → Color** gives a show an accent color (Teal, Blue, Purple, Pink, Coral, Amber or Green), so you can see at a glance which show is loaded. It saves right away.
- **Shows → Import Show from Another Profile** copies a saved show from another profile on this computer. Your copy is yours to change.

### See what you've played

- A **▶ count** appears next to every track you've played tonight, in the hotkeys, the holding tank and search results.
- **Clearing counts:** right-click a song and choose **Mark as Unplayed** to clear one, or use **Shows → Mark All Songs as Unplayed** to clear them all.
- **Counts are for tonight only.** They start fresh each time the profile opens, carry across show switches, and come back after a crash restore.

### Safer during a show

- **Crash recovery:** if Mx. Voice doesn't close normally, the next launch asks whether to **restore the show in progress**. Press Return to restore it. Choosing "Open saved show" keeps a copy aside instead of deleting it.
- **If the window crashes** but the app doesn't, Mx. Voice offers to reload it and restores your show.
- **Closing the window now asks before quitting,** so a stray click can't stop the music. Quit (Exit on Windows) in the menu still quits right away.
- **Esc in a dialog** now just closes the dialog and leaves the music playing.

### One-handed fades

- **Tap Esc** to cut the music, as before. **Hold Esc** to fade it out. Press Esc again during a fade to cut it. Shift+Esc still fades.
- **Fades use the length set in Preferences,** and each profile keeps the fade length it used before the update.

### Tabs and files

- **Each tab row has a ⋯ menu** (or right-click a tab) to rename the tab, clear it, **Save Tab to File…** or **Load Tab from File…**. Clear empties only that tab; the songs stay in your library.
- **Tab files** keep one tab as a file you can load into any show, like a pre-show playlist or a standard set of drops. They're the same `.mrv` and `.hld` files older versions use. Loading into a tab that has songs asks first.
- **The Hotkeys menu** (and its Cmd/Ctrl-O and Cmd/Ctrl-S shortcuts) and the folder and save icons are gone. Use Shows → Save for the show, and each tab's ⋯ menu for files.
- **Still have your real show in an old hotkey or holding-tank file?** Load it onto the tabs with Load Tab from File…, then use Save or Save As.

### Profiles

- **Switch Profile no longer restarts the app.** It opens right in the window: your current profile is grayed out, and Esc takes you back.
- **Older profile backups have no shows.** Restoring one turns its layout into a show named Default Show. That layout may be from the middle of a show rather than a prepared one. The restore confirmation tells you which shows a backup contains.

### A fresher look

- **New look throughout:** cleaner type, song rows with the title over the artist and the time on the right, calmer F-key badges, flatter panels, a new player bar and a quieter search table.
- **On macOS,** the window uses an inset title bar.
- **A What's New tour** walks you through the changes the first time you open 4.4.

### Please help us test

These are the areas we'd most like you to try:

- **Your existing layout:** after upgrading, check that it opened as **Default Show** with all your hotkeys and holding-tank songs.
- **Your normal show flow:** build or change a layout, **Save**, quit and reopen, and check it comes back as saved.
- **Shows:** Save As, switching between shows (with and without unsaved changes), Revert to Saved, Rename, Delete and Import Show.
- **Crash recovery:** force-quit Mx. Voice mid-show, reopen it, and restore.
- **Tab files:** load an old `.mrv` or `.hld` file through a tab's ⋯ menu.
- **Esc:** tap Esc to cut and hold it to fade during playback.
- **Played counts:** check the ▶ counts as you play, and clear them.
- **Profiles:** switch profiles, and restore a profile backup.

Report problems at https://github.com/minter/mxvoice-electron/issues. Include what you did, what you expected, and your operating system.
